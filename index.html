About Beyond Realms

Intuition is your inner compass.

Smiti Jani · Founder · London

Beyond Realms is led by Smiti Jani, who has worked with tarot, astrology, Reiki and intuitive guidance since 2009 — supporting people through transition, uncertainty and emotional complexity.

Her work is centred on clarity: not prediction for curiosity, but understanding for direction. She helps clients recognise the deeper patterns behind their experiences, so they can make decisions from a clearer internal place. Each session blends intuitive reading with grounded interpretation, holding space for both emotional understanding and practical awareness.

Sessions are held via WhatsApp video call — available worldwide.
